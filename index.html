About Us

About PSA

PSA Koblenz is a student association and a non-profit organization that aims to facilitate Pakistani students and community in Koblenz. The primary objective of PSA is to help and guide Pakistani students during the course of their stay — supporting them with studies, health, accommodation, internships and jobs.

We also assist Pakistani students coming to Koblenz from other cities for internships or thesis. PSA aims to build a community bound by inclusivity and cohesion, organizing cultural shows, sports activities, excursion trips and friendly gatherings each year. In short, PSA Koblenz is a proud propagator of Pakistan's culture, tradition, language and food.
